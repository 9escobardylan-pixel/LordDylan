The process

The same three stages behind every ad I write.

01

Listen first.

I dig through the places your buyers speak freely: reviews, Reddit threads, comments, and lift the exact phrases they use when nobody's pitching them.

02

Engineer the hook.

That real customer language goes into AI tooling I've dialed in for ad writing, and out come hooks that land like they were pulled straight from your buyer's head.

03

Build the stopper.

The strongest hook becomes a finished static or AI-video ad, almost annoying to scroll past, ready to launch the same day I hand it over.
